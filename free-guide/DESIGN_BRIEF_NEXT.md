# BASEFROM50 — Design brief for next edit

Status: active redesign direction.

## Canonical visual references
Use the previously generated ULTIMATE GUIDE images in the user's Library as the north star:
- `/БАЗА/Навигация: Ultimate Guide.png`
- `/БАЗА/Навигация Base From 50.png`
- `/БАЗА/Премиум-гайд по закупкам и доставке.png`
- `/БАЗА/Роскошный гид по закупкам и доставке.png`

These references are more important than the A1/A2/A3 experiments.

## Recovered accepted ULTIMATE style
- unified dark / premium BASE FROM 50 aesthetic;
- black / white / dark-neutral base with restrained metallic/gold accents;
- large, highly readable typography;
- the visual treatment of the word `ULTIMATE` was specifically liked;
- centered / strongly composed headline treatment;
- minimalist background;
- no block number as a decorative hero element;
- no tiny bottom copy;
- no unnecessary drawn cards/boxes just to fill the frame;
- all visuals should feel like one premium series with the same format and compositional logic;
- historically the Telegram visual format was 1600×900 (16:9); for the PDF, translate the same art direction into A4 portrait without copying the 16:9 layout literally.

## Important correction from previous attempts
The A1/A2/A3 concepts drifted away from the established ULTIMATE identity:
- A1/A2 became too Swiss/technical and black-white;
- A3 became beige/fashion-editorial and too soft;
- do NOT continue the cream-paper/serif-luxury direction.

## Typography direction
Do not invent a new unrelated luxury serif system.
Reconstruct the feel of the ULTIMATE visuals:
- display typography: bold, premium, high-impact, spacious enough not to merge letters;
- very large headline sizes;
- high readability on phone;
- body: clean neutral sans;
- strong Cyrillic support;
- maximum two families;
- the word `ULTIMATE` / key statements should carry the strongest typographic personality.

Exact typeface names used by image generation were not reliably preserved, so match the visual character rather than falsely claiming an exact font.

## Color direction
Use the recovered ULTIMATE visual language:
- dominant: deep black / charcoal / dark premium neutral;
- text: off-white / white;
- secondary: gray / silver;
- accent: restrained gold / warm metallic highlight;
- accent should remain sparse;
- no muddy beige field;
- no red/orange gradient unless directly present in a canonical reference being matched.

## Background direction
Use minimalist premium backgrounds inspired by the canonical ULTIMATE generated images:
- dark, controlled, atmospheric;
- subtle depth / luxury texture / abstract lighting rather than flat CSS gray;
- do not rely on busy stock photography;
- background must support the headline, not compete with it.

## Process
1. Re-open the four canonical ULTIMATE reference images before editing.
2. Audit the current PDF against them.
3. Rebuild pages 1 and 5 only.
4. Keep typography much larger than A3.
5. Render at full page and contact-sheet scale.
6. Compare against the canonical ULTIMATE images, not against A1/A2/A3.
7. Only after the direction matches, propagate it to the rest of the guide.
